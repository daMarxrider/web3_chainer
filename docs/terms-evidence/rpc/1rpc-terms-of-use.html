2. Description of our Services

2.1 About 1RPC. 1RPC is a RPC relay that operates as a proxy layer to shield user data prior to its transmission to existing RPC endpoints. It effectively eliminates any potential for metadata exposure and leakage, and can be deployed by users with just one click.

2.2 About 1RPC+. Apart from existing functionalities on 1RPC, 1RPC+ enables the usage of transaction sanitizers that are specifically designed to prevent phishing attempts or other forms of fraudulent activities.

2.3 About Transaction Sanitizers. Transaction sanitizers are custom rules that define the ways users interact with the blockchain. These include, but are not limited to, user-created blacklist or whitelist address databases, recipient validation on Uniswap, address scanning enabled by third-party service providers, and explorer contract verification on official blockchain explorers.

By using 1RPC+ and its transaction sanitizers, users acknowledge and agree that the Relay is designed to prevent potentially fraudulent or malicious transactions from being executed.
